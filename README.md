# ChestGlow

A yellow glow and sparks on the caches in **Azeroth Survivors**, with a minimap button and a settings panel.

Caches you can afford glow. Caches you cannot buy yet stay dim. A notice appears the moment your gold covers the next one.

Needs game **0.139.2** or newer (world anchors). Works alongside SurvivorsDragonUi: when that addon already draws the chest icon, ChestGlow draws only the glow behind it.

## Features

- **Glow:** a yellow halo and sparks on each cache in the world, including dungeons and raids.
- **Only when you can afford it:** the glow stays off until your gold covers the next cache.
- **Dim icons you can't afford:** a cache you cannot buy yet is drawn at about half strength.
- **Announce when you can buy one:** a notice appears the moment you have enough gold.
- **Range:** glow only out to 30, 45, 60, or 90 yards, or leave it on All.
- **Size:** small, medium, or large.
- **Minimap button:** left-click opens the panel, right-click turns the glow on or off, drag moves the button. It works on round and square minimaps.
- The panel stays under the level-up cards and remembers where you put it.

## Install

From SurvivorsForge, install it with the launcher.

From GitHub, download `ChestGlow.zip` from [Releases](../../releases), put the `ChestGlow` folder into `benilla-config/AddOns/`, and restart the game.

## Commands

- `/chestglow`: open or close the panel
- `/chestglow on`: turn the glow on
- `/chestglow off`: turn the glow off
- `/chestglow toggle`: switch the glow
